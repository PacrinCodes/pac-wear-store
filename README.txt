PAC WEAR — Starter Storefront
=============================

Open index.html in a browser to preview the responsive storefront.

Included:
- Mobile-responsive streetwear landing page
- Collection category filters
- Product cards with editable sample products/prices
- Functional demo shopping bag and subtotal
- Email sign-up interaction (demo only)

Important before selling:
- Replace placeholder product mockups with real product photography.
- Verify brand name/trademark availability before investing in labels or packaging.
- Connect a real checkout/payment provider and order-management workflow.
- Connect email capture to a service; the current form does not store addresses.
- Add shipping, returns, privacy, terms, contact details and tax information appropriate to your business.
- The prices and products are examples only, not confirmed inventory.
- This is a static prototype, not a hosted/live store.
